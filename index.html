03 / Proposed Solution

Learning Pathway

The program could be separated into different levels so young people can gradually develop their knowledge.

LEVEL 01

Water Awareness

Learn basic water-safety rules, recognise hazards and understand safer behaviour around pools and beaches.

LEVEL 02

Survival Basics

Introduce basic concepts such as floating, staying calm and getting help from a safe location.

LEVEL 03

Helping Others

Learn how to alert an adult or emergency services and why people should not put themselves in danger during a rescue.

Research

Find out what young people already know about water safety and what information they need.

Design

Create short online lessons with visuals, activities and quizzes.

Test

Ask students and community members for feedback on clarity, usefulness and accessibility.

Improve & Share

Improve the program and share it through suitable school and community channels.

05 / Project Analysis

SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • Schools and community groups can promote the program.
  • Local pools and recreation facilities already exist.
  • Young people can engage with digital content.
  • Water safety is already recognised as important.

Weaknesses

  • Some learners may lack basic swimming skills.
  • Fear of water can affect participation.
  • Online education cannot replace practical instruction.
  • Some families may have limited access to facilities.

Opportunities

  • Partner with swimming teachers or aquatic centres.
  • Run water-safety workshops.
  • Use social media to spread awareness.
  • Create different levels of learning.

Threats

  • Unsafe behaviour around water.
  • Low attendance or engagement.
  • Limited funding or equipment.
  • Bad weather or limited facility access.
